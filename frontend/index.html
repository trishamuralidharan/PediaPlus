⚡ Quick First Aid Tips
🤒Fever >101°F: Give paracetamol, sponge with lukewarm water, call doctor if >103°F or lasts >3 days
🍎Choking: Give 5 back blows between shoulder blades, then 5 abdominal thrusts. Call 108 immediately
🤕Head Injury: Keep child still, apply cold compress, watch for vomiting/confusion — rush to ER
🐝Allergic Reaction: Remove allergen, use prescribed epi-pen if available, call 108 for anaphylaxis
